Context

  • Joined MaintainX as roughly the 350th employee and quickly ramped up on the existing SaaS stack and RBAC configuration already in place. Months later, the company adopted OKTA as its Identity Provider, requiring a full reassessment of how identity should flow across the stack.

What I Did

  • Redesigned identity flow around OKTA, building RBAC-driven dynamic group provisioning by department, role, and location, with Hibob (our HRIS) as the identity source-of-truth
  • Implemented SCIM provisioning for a cleaner, automated user lifecycle process, and deployed push-group provisioning for SaaS applications that supported it — running OKTA and Google Groups in parallel to keep native Workspace integrations (Drive sharing, etc.) working seamlessly
  • Completed a company-wide SSO rollout across both Google Workspace and OKTA over roughly a year with zero operational disruption
  • Evaluated our SaaS-management tooling as part of the same audit and moved from Torii to 1Password's SaaS Manager (formerly Trelica) after finding Torii's OKTA/SCIM integration unreliable — same cost, meaningfully better feature set

Impact

  • Delivered easier and more secure authentication for every employee by cutting the number of separate accounts and passwords they had to track in password managers, simplifying day-to-day login workflows. Scaled OKTA to manage and oversee 180+ SaaS applications company-wide under stronger, centralized identity management. The same license-tier audit also surfaced rarely-used seats to cut — ~$50K saved from an Atlassian license cleanup alone, with a similar pass on Figma.
