About Me

I'm an IT Team Lead and Systems Specialist with over a decade of hands-on experience, starting with hardware repair and working my way up to leading identity, security, and infrastructure strategy for fast-scaling companies. That path took me from fixing devices at Microbytes and hosting new-Mac-buyer onboarding sessions at Apple, through help desk and systems administration roles, to leading IT teams across Canada and the US. I care about building IT systems that scale with a company, not against it: automating the repetitive, securing the essentials, and giving teams tools that get out of their way. I work daily across English, French, Armenian, and Arabic, which comes in handy supporting distributed teams. Outside of work, I run an extensive home lab (virtualization, networking, self-hosted AI) because I like understanding infrastructure from the ground up, not just administering it.
